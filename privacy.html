Asteroid

Privacy

Last updated September 28, 2026

Asteroid keeps your business's records: your clients, their addresses and contacts, orders, invoices and payments, and your messengers and their pay. This page explains where those records live and who can see them.

Asteroid Desktop

Your records stay on your computer, inside your web browser, and in the backup folder you choose. We don't receive them. When Asteroid opens it checks our website for a newer version; that request carries no records, only what any web page request does (such as your computer's internet address), and our website host, GitHub, may log it.

Asteroid Online

Your records are stored in a PostgreSQL database run by our hosting provider, Render, in the United States. Connections are encrypted. Only people you give a login to can see them. We access them only to set up your account or when you ask us for help. The database is backed up daily. If you turn on summary or invoice emails, they are sent through the email account you connect.

What we never do

  • Sell or rent your records, or your clients' details.
  • Use them for advertising.
  • Share them, except with the hosting provider that stores them, or when the law requires it.

When you email us

We keep your emails so we can help you and keep a history of your account.

Leaving

You can save a complete backup of your records at any time. When an Asteroid Online account closes, we send a final backup and delete the hosted records within 30 days.
